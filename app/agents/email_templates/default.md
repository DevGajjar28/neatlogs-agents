# Default outbound style

SignalSDR uses this as the system default email style. Templates guide voice and structure; evidence rules, opt-out, and send limits are enforced in code and cannot be turned off.

## Voice

- Short, direct, peer-to-peer. No flattery, no "hope this finds you well".
- One clear reason for reaching out, grounded only in sourced facts.
- No invented metrics, customers, or news. If you cannot prove it, do not say it.
- Never put evidence ids, brackets, or citation markers in the subject or body.

## Placeholders

Use when natural: `{{first_name}}`, `{{company}}`, `{{role}}`, `{{seller_name}}`, `{{sender_first_name}}`.

## Email 1 (first touch)

- Max ~120 words.
- Open with a concrete observation about their company (from evidence).
- Tie that observation to one seller value prop.
- Soft ask for a short reply or call — not a calendar dump.
- End with the SignalSDR opt-out line (added/enforced automatically).

## Follow-up 1

- Max ~70 words.
- Add **one new** evidence-backed point. Do not "just bump".
- Keep the same thread intent.

## Follow-up 2

- Max ~70 words.
- Final new angle or a crisp close. Still no empty bumps.

## Banned phrases (warn in approval)

- guaranteed, game-changer, revolutionary, synergy, circle back, touch base
- Em dash (—) as a stylistic crutch

## Example (illustrative only)

Subject: Scaling agent workloads without surprise infra cost

Hi {{first_name}},

{{company}}'s recent push on agent tooling usually means inference and orchestration spend grows faster than headcount. {{seller_name}} helps teams keep that spend predictable without slowing shipping.

Worth a 15-minute look this week?

If this isn't relevant, reply "stop" and I won't email again.
