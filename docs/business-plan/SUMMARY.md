## What to do in cfo.ai

1. Sign up via https://cfo.ai/neathacks → **cancel subscription immediately** (04 §3).
2. Enter costs from `measured-inputs.md` (Crustdata, Bedrock, infra).
3. Enter pricing options $49/100, $99/100, $0.80/lead.
4. Build base / growth / conservative scenarios.
5. Export PDF + 2–3 screenshots here as `cfo-plan.pdf`, `cfo-costs.png`, `cfo-runway.png`.

## Narrative for the demo (≤20 s)

> We measured **5.3 Crustdata credits per qualified lead** on the v1 PostHog run — about a third of v0’s 14.6 — because of caching, enrich-survivors-only, and the contact fallback chain. At five cents a credit that’s roughly **27 cents** of data cost; a **$49/100 leads** plan still clears margin before LLM, and we model runway in cfo.ai from those measured costs, not guesses.
